## Overview

Honeyguide is a mobile app concept for planning solo time, meeting people through shared activities, and staying connected with a local community. The current prototype is set in Sydney and brings a daily itinerary, nearby activity matching, and messaging into one place.

[Open the interactive Honeyguide mockup]({{ "/honeyguide-mockup.html" | relative_url }})

## Plan your day

The **Plan** screen presents a timeline from 8 AM to 6 PM. Activities are visually separated into three types: solo plans, plans with other people, and free time.

The sample day includes:

- **Sydney Opera House tour** — a solo plan from 9:00–10:30 at Bennelong Point.
- **Meet & Wine** — a joined activity with four people from 12:30–2:00 at Barangaroo.
- **Free time** — an open block from 2:00–4:00 where a plan can be added manually or suggested by Honeyguide.
- **Language + coffee chat** — a joined activity with six people from 4:00–5:30, covering English, Vietnamese, and Chinese.

For the free-time block, the prototype can add a blank activity or generate a Honeyguide suggestion. Its example suggestion is a **Harbour sketch walk** from 2:30–3:30, with three nearby members interested.

## Find activity matches

The **Matches** screen collects activities around Sydney. They can be filtered by **All**, **Hosted by me**, or **Joined**.

The prototype includes three example activities:

- **Language + coffee chat** in Surry Hills, hosted by the user with six people attending.
- **Meet & Wine** in Barangaroo, hosted by Phuong and presented as a joined event in English and Vietnamese.
- **Paint & Walk** at The Rocks, hosted by Yuki with ten spots and support for English and Japanese.

People can join or leave an activity, while hosts have a separate option to manage their own event. Joining an activity adds it to the itinerary.

## Stay connected

The **Messages** screen switches between direct conversations and local community threads.

The direct-message examples are:

- **Phuong** — Sydney; English, Vietnamese, and Chinese.
- **John** — Sydney; English and German.
- **Yuki** — Sydney; English, Japanese, and Chinese.

The local community includes:

- **#sydney-newcomers** for local questions such as finding a quiet coworking café.
- **#language-exchange** for organising language practice.
- **#weekend-plans** for activities such as a Saturday coastal walk.

## Current prototype

The current version is an interactive front-end concept rather than a finished production app. It demonstrates:

- Navigation between Plan, Matches, and Messages.
- Activity filters and join/leave states.
- Direct messages and local-thread views.
- Manual and AI-assisted additions to free time.
- A mobile-first visual style using a warm honey palette.

More product, technical, and release details will be added as Honeyguide develops.
